## Stage 1 — `habit_core.py` (pure stdlib)
- SQLite WAL schema (`habits`, `checkins`, `readings`, `events`, `meta`);
  cadences `daily|weekdays|weekly:N|interval:Nh`; streaks with open-period
  grace (today never breaks a streak); capacity modes GREEN/YELLOW/RED with
  per-habit effort tiers; CLI with `--json` on every machine-readable verb.
- Selftest earned its keep — caught three real bugs:
  1. `int()` truncates toward zero → wrong period bucket for timestamps
     before the anchor. Fix: floor division.
  2. Streak walk clamped to habit creation → legitimate backfill invisible.
     Fix: removed clamp; imports backdate via `add --created`.
  3. Windows holds `-wal`/`-shm` briefly after close → selftest cleanup
     made best-effort.
- Two test *expectations* were wrong, not the code: a weekday streak needs
  every weekday checked (unchecked Mon–Wed breaks the chain); an unchecked
  today is grace, not a break. Rewrote scenarios to model reality.
- 19/19 green. Committed.
